# Adeel Gulzar – FP&A Portfolio

## Financial Planning & Analysis | Financial Modeling | Business Partnering

Welcome to my FP&A portfolio.

This repository showcases practical Financial Planning & Analysis, financial modeling, management reporting, budgeting, forecasting, variance analysis, and business partnering projects developed using Microsoft Excel.

The projects demonstrate how financial data can be transformed into meaningful insights to support management decision-making.

---

## Portfolio Projects

### 01. Balance Sheet & Cash Flow Analysis

Financial statement analysis model covering Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, liquidity, and working capital analysis.

**Key skills:** Financial Statement Analysis | Cash Flow Analysis | Working Capital | FP&A Reporting

### 02. IT Projects CAPEX Tracker

CAPEX tracking model designed to monitor IT project budgets, actual spending, budget utilization, and project-level variances.

**Key skills:** CAPEX Planning | Budget Management | Variance Analysis | Project Financial Management

---

### 03. Marketing Expense Analysis

Marketing expense analysis model comparing actual spending against budget and identifying significant variances and spending trends.

**Key skills:** OPEX Analysis | Budget vs Actual | Variance Analysis | Expense Management

---

### 04. Stores Executive Dashboard

Executive retail dashboard providing a consolidated view of store sales, profitability, expenses, KPIs, and performance trends.

**Key skills:** Retail FP&A | KPI Analysis | Dashboard Development | Store Performance | Management Reporting

---

### 05. Sales Performance Analysis

Sales analysis model evaluating sales performance against targets, trends, variances, and key business drivers.

**Key skills:** Sales Analysis | Commercial FP&A | KPI Analysis | Performance Management

---

### 06. Store Maintenance Expense Analysis

Store maintenance cost analysis designed to identify spending trends, budget variances, high-cost locations, and potential cost-saving opportunities.

**Key skills:** OPEX Analysis | Cost Management | Variance Analysis | Cost Optimization

---

### 07. Store-Level P&L Analysis

Store-level Profit & Loss model analyzing revenue, gross profit, operating expenses, profitability, and budget variances.

**Key skills:** P&L Analysis | Profitability Analysis | Retail FP&A | Budget vs Actual | Variance Analysis

---

### 08. Store P&L Executive Summary

Executive-level P&L summary providing management with a consolidated view of sales, margins, operating expenses, profitability, and financial variances across the store network.

**Key skills:** Executive Reporting | P&L Analysis | Profitability Analysis | KPI Analysis | FP&A
